# Our Approach

Your Trusted Partner for Board Exam Preparation

At Online Math Academy, we provide educational resources, notes, solved examples, MCQs and board-exam preparation support for students of Classes 9 and 10.

Our resources are designed around the current curriculum and focus on conceptual clarity, step-by-step solutions, practice questions and exam-smart revision.

Mathematics resources include worked solutions and extra practice. Physics focuses on concepts and numericals, Chemistry on reactions and problem-solving, while Biology resources include diagrams, definitions and important questions.
